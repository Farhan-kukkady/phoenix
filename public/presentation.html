Problem Statements 1 & 2

Catalog Paralysis & The Technical Jargon Wall

1

Problem #1: Catalog Paralysis

"I don't know where to find what I need—there are too many products."

With 21 international brands (X-Rite, GMG, GlobalVision, Valiani, Hanatek) and over 200 specialized products, customers spend minutes clicking menus, get overwhelmed, and leave without asking for help.

2

Problem #2: The Technical Jargon Wall

"Which optical geometry do I need for my substrate?"

Packaging managers often don't know whether flexible plastic film requires a 45°:0° with M3 polarization or a d/8° sphere. Because a wrong guess costs $20,000, they freeze and delay their decision.

Result: Phoenix was losing high-intent buyers who couldn't navigate the catalog on their own.

"Why should our plant spend $18,000 on this machine?"

Factory CFOs don't care about "nanometers" or "apertures"—they care about cashflow and waste. Without showing how many cardboard sheets and press hours are saved, quotes face immense executive resistance.

Problem #4: The 24/7 Regional Reality

"We need answers now during our night packaging shift."

Buyers across Saudi Arabia, Kuwait, Egypt, and the GCC operate outside standard Dubai office hours. A customer searching at 9:00 PM will not wait until the next morning for basic answers.

Opportunity: AI provides instant numbers and technical reassurance 24 hours a day.
